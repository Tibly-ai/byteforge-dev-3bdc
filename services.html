The process

How a project actually goes.

01

Discovery

A call about your business, your users, and what the site needs to actually do.

02

Scope & Quote

A written proposal covering pages, features, timeline, and a fixed price. No hourly surprises.

03

Design

Wireframes and a visual direction, refined with your feedback before a single line of code is written.

04

Build

Development happens in the open. You get a staging link and can watch progress as it happens.

05

Launch & Support

Deployment, DNS, and testing handled, then I stick around for fixes and updates after you're live.
